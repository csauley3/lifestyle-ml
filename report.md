**Project Definition**

The dataset is a 2022 annual CDC survey data of 400k+ adults related to their health status. It features general lifestyle habits and binary disease features.

Our target and learning objective is to see if there is a correlation between lifestyle habits and disease indication.

The task will mainly be classification, but there will be a few regression models for the the lifestyle features.

The dataset is quite large so computational time may be long. Data processing will be an important feature for us.
